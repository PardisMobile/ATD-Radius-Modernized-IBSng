## Naming rule
The source contains historical capitalization variants (RAS/Ras, IPPool/IPpool, singular/plural variants). When implementing a specific page, use the exact title or label from its corresponding A1.24 source template rather than inventing a normalized ATD name.

Technical filenames and CSS/JavaScript identifiers may use implementation-specific names. Operator-facing terminology may not.
